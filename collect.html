ACTField

A.T. field data collection

Capture invasive plants, forest pests, hazard trees, trail and facility conditions, grassland checks and treatment areas along the Appalachian Trail, on a phone, tablet or computer. It runs in GeoLibre, a free, open-source GIS, over the same trail, facility and land layers as the ACT dashboard.

Collect in the field

  1. Open the project (button above, or in the GeoLibre app: Project → Open From → URL… and paste the address above).
  2. Before you lose signal, zoom to your section and choose Project → Offline Basemap… to cache the map.
  3. Choose Controls → Field collection…, pick a layer, then Use GPS or Pick on map.
  4. Fill in the form and add photos. Date, record ID, GPS accuracy and whether the point came from GPS fill in for you.
  5. Close the dialog between records; the Open Field Collection pill keeps your session.

Send your records back. Records are kept in the project on your device. At the end of the day use Project → Save As… to keep the whole project, or a layer's ⋯ → Export to send GeoJSON, CSV, Shapefile or GeoPackage to the resource office.

Forms

  • Invasive plant observationsSpecies, cover class, patch size, phenology, treatment
  • Forest pest and diseaseHWA, SLF, EAB, BLD and others; host, crown, severity
  • Hazard treesISA risk matrix scores itself; bat roost potential
  • Trail condition issuesErosion, drainage, blowdowns, blazes; severity and crew
  • Facility conditionShelters, privies, campsites, bridges, springs
  • Grassland field checksUse, mowing, woody and invasive cover, birds, AgSUP
  • Treatment and survey areasPolygons; method, product, EPA Reg. No., effort

Reference layers

A.T. centerline, side trails, shelters, campsites, parking, privies, bridges and vistas (ANST services), administrative areas by ownership, maintained landscapes and states, with USGS imagery, USGS shaded relief and OpenTopoMap under them. Facility and trail layers load for the area in view.

Get the GeoLibre app

The project also opens in the GeoLibre apps, which use the device GPS (and, on Android, a Bluetooth GNSS receiver with RTK corrections): iPhone and iPad · Android · Windows, macOS, Linux.
